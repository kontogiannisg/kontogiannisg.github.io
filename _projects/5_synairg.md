Research Scientist since 2022 on the EU Horizon project [**SynAir-G**](https://www.synairg.eu/),
_Disrupting Noxious Synergies of Indoor Air Pollutants and their Impact in Childhood Health
and Wellbeing, using Advanced Intelligent Multisensing and Green Interventions_ (Grant
Agreement No. 101057271).

I work on the interactions between indoor air quality measurements and physiological
signals from wearable devices, using multivariate and temporal causal models to study their
combined effect on children's health and wellbeing.
