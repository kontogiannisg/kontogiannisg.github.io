Research contract (since February 2026) with the Computer Technology Institute and Press
"Diophantus" (CTI), which publishes and distributes Greece's school textbooks. Distribution
planning depends on accurate per-school student forecasts, and these are hard to get:
demographics shift between regions, and students change schools, often across
municipalities, for political and socioeconomic reasons. The goal is to plan logistics and
textbook distribution around these forecasts. I am building the following, for integration
into the IT infrastructure of CTI's Publications Directorate:

- forecasting models for student population, textbook demand and inventory;
- logistics simulation and what-if supply-chain scenarios.
